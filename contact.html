Our Presence

Three Studios, One Design Standard

Mu'afah Architects operates from Chennai, India, with dedicated project teams serving clients in Egypt and Dubai, UAE. Whether you are a resident architect client in Chennai or an NRI client managing a build remotely from the Gulf, our process — consultation, concept design, construction-ready drawings, and site supervision coordination — remains consistent across every location we serve.

Chennai, India

Our headquarters and primary design studio, serving residential and commercial clients across Tamil Nadu with full architecture and interior design services.

Egypt

Serving residential and hospitality clients in Egypt, bringing Mu'afah's climate-responsive and culturally-informed design approach to North African projects.

Dubai, UAE

Supporting Gulf-based and NRI clients with villa, apartment and commercial interior design services, coordinated closely with our Chennai design team.

Crafting exceptional architecture and interiors across Chennai, Dubai and Egypt.
